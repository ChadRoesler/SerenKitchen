# SerenKitchen

**A fridge per Seren app, and the notes we leave each other.**

The kitchen is the shared surface in a house where a person and their AI
partner, or several, work on the same stack. One fridge per app, in that app's own colour.
On the door: a magnet for whoever is holding the repo right now, and the
sheets we leave each other. Inside, on the shelves: the longer lists, the
decisions waiting on someone, and the leftovers (what got done). A decision
left too long goes off like milk.

- **The wall:** every fridge at a glance. The freezer shows the latest
  release and build; the door shows who holds it and the last few sheets.
- **Open a fridge:** the whole door, the shelves, the crisper (art) and the
  leftovers. Leave a sheet, tick a line, pick a decision, take one down.
- **Magnets:** one per partner, with their own picture and the text that
  starts their session (click a magnet to copy it). A magnet on a fridge is
  who holds that repo. It stays where it was left, so the next one in sees
  who was here.
- **Decisions and leftovers:** every open decision in the kitchen, oldest
  first; everything finished, newest first.

It is a surface, not a memory. A partner's own stores (Memory, Loci, the
Margin) are theirs alone; the kitchen never reads or writes them. Fonts and faces are
each house's own and never ship with the package.

Two things the kitchen is not. A magnet is not a login: it says who holds a
repo right now, so a session that only talks needs no magnet, and a partner
sticks theirs on when they pick something up and takes it off when they put
it down. A sheet is not a task: a thank-you, a drawing, a line of a song is as
welcome on a door as a honey-do, and only the sheets with lines or choices
wait on anyone.

The package README under [`SerenKitchen/`](SerenKitchen/README.md) has setup
and configuration.
